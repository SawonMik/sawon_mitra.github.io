Remanufacturing, Platform Governance, and Carbon Taxation: Aligning Contract Choice with Environmental Performance

Sawon Mitra,Abhinav Chakraborty, Abhishek Chakraborty

Under Review at Journal of Remanufacturing

This study develops a game-theoretic model of a closed-loop supply chain where a manufacturer sells new and remanufactured products via an online platform under reselling or agency contracts, subject to a government carbon tax. It finds that agency contracts outperform reselling contracts on price, emissions, and social welfare, but high consumer environmental sensitivity can push manufacturers toward reselling despite its inferior outcomes. The results highlight platform contract structure as an implicit policy lever that shapes the effectiveness of environmental regulation.
